# Eclipse materials

The item uses `assets/godwand/textures/item/eclipse_materials.png`, a grayscale atlas tinted by the renderer. Model UVs use a logical 128 by 128 canvas. Each 64 by 64 quadrant holds one material: obsidian at the upper left, worn gold at the upper right, leather at the lower left, and pale energy at the lower right. The energy sphere uses its own quadrant rather than the stone grain.

The crescent has continuous shared cross-sections, with two broad raised areas on its front and slightly unequal thickness. Its inner gold edge stays smooth. The renderer keeps an opaque, full-bright sphere underneath two translucent aura layers; shaderpack bloom can change their appearance. This does not add dynamic light to nearby terrain.

The neck overlaps the shaft and ends inside the blade with a closed upper cap. A thin tilted energy ring frames the larger sphere; the enlarged satellite travels in that ring's plane. This uses the supplied staff reference's idea of a ring around the core while keeping Eclipse's crescent silhouette.

The blade's outer body is thicker and has a raised central spine on both faces. Two raised gold neck bands emphasize the curved transition into the blade. Supernova uses the neutral item-use pose and the wand's own restrained charge/release motion, avoiding vanilla's spear pose pulling the long staff into the camera.

The atlas was generated with the built-in Imagegen tool using this prompt:

> Create ONE production-ready seamless grayscale albedo material atlas for a Minecraft custom 3D magical staff. Square image, orthographic flat texture, exactly FOUR equal square tiles in a 2 by 2 grid, no margins, no gutters, no borders, no text, no labels, no objects, no lighting gradients, no perspective, no diagrams. Each quadrant independently seamless. This will be multiplied by dark obsidian, gold, red leather and violet energy vertex colors IN CODE, so EVERY tile must be LIGHT NEUTRAL GRAYSCALE, predominantly RGB 205-245, with readable darker details in RGB 125-185, never colored, never black overall. Upper-left quadrant: restrained obsidian forged volcanic stone, fine angular fractured mineral veins and subtle broad uneven strata, a handful of brighter worn ridges, predominantly light gray, NOT a random noisy gravel pile. Upper-right quadrant: hammered aged metal, small uneven shallow dents, a few short hand-cut engraved scratches, selective bright scuffed edges, mostly pale gray, not rust. Lower-left quadrant: fine stretched leather grain, subtle fibrous creases, tiny irregular pores and occasional short wear scratches, mostly light neutral gray, no straps or borders and no drawn stitches. Lower-right quadrant: near white softly swirling energy mist, very faint flowing wisps on almost white background, no stars, no black voids, neutral white grayscale. Pixel-art game texture style at the equivalent of 64x64 texels PER QUADRANT, crisp deliberate pixel clusters and restrained detail, no photorealistic pores, no noisy AI marbling. Keep enough contrast for textures to remain readable when tinted dark but retain a clean intentional crafted look. Strict square 2x2 atlas, each material fills its quadrant edge to edge. This is the raw bitmap used by the engine, not a presentation of a texture.
