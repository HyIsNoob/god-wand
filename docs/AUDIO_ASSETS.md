Current mixes were supplied by the owner as CC0-only replacements. This records the owner's designation, not an independent rights audit. Encoded from MP3 to OGG Vorbis without trimming, normalization or timing edits.

Atomic charge starts on cast; blast starts on the RELEASE packet at the release flash, before the explosion. Charge continues through hold, and audio tails finish normally; cancellation and world reset stop playback. The heartbeat uses vanilla Minecraft audio.

Previous audio is retained in a local backup outside the repository and is not included in new JARs.
