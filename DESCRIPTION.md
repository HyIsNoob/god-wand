# God Wand

[Wiki, controls and guides](https://god-wand-wiki.vercel.app/)

**One wand. Seven skills. Choose the spell, charge it, and decide when to release.**

Walk through mountains, pull enemies into a gravity orb, raise a drill from the ground, hold an Atomic blast until you are ready to let it go, or draw a burning bow and fire Fuga's phoenix arrow.

**⚠️ FLASH WARNING ⚠️**

This mod contains intense flashing lights and visual effects that may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Player discretion is advised.

## Seven skills

### 1. Spatial Path

Open a temporary path through solid terrain. The blocks return behind you. Use its shield against incoming projectiles, or form a temporary bridge across a gap.

### 2. Singularity

Hold right-click to charge a gravity orb, then release to launch it. It pulls in nearby enemies and blocks before collapsing. Right-click again to trigger the collapse early. Displaced terrain returns afterward.

### 3. Earth Drill

Gather nearby blocks into a spinning drill, then drive it into the ground. Server settings let you keep the block lift while turning off permanent terrain damage.

### 4. Supernova

Hold right-click for seven seconds to unleash a black hole and a large explosion. Server owners can allow the block pull, the terrain destruction, both or neither.

### 5. Stone Cannon

Charge a stone projectile and fire it through enemies. Block destruction on impact can be turned off separately.

### 6. I am Atomic

Hold right-click as purple lines spread from your feet and build a fortress around you. Energy gathers into your sword as the camera moves from the ground to the closing roof, then returns to your character.

**I AM. ATOMIC.**

The fortress compresses. The screen goes dark. A heartbeat repeats while the spell waits for your release.

**Let go to detonate.** A column of light expands across the area, with outward shockwaves and purple wind. After it contracts, glowing marks remain on the ground for 30 seconds before fading.

Atomic includes its own camera sequence, character poses and sound effects. Your movement is locked and incoming damage is blocked while casting. Once the explosion begins, skipping the camera lets you move again. Release before the initial charge finishes to cancel.

### 7. Fuga

Hold right-click to gather fire between your palms. Clap them together and a burning vortex rises around you as you draw a bow made of flame. Stand still, aim, then release. Letting go before the arrow is ready cancels the cast.

The fire arrow becomes a gliding phoenix. When it hits, the phoenix rises into the sky, a blazing meteor falls and a pillar of fire erupts, setting nearby enemies and flammable blocks alight and leaving the ground glowing red-hot. Enemies caught under the meteor are slowed until it explodes.

Fuga has its own cutscene, which you can turn off in the settings, and its own soundtrack. Server owners can adjust its damage, cooldown and terrain destruction.

## Pick your wand's appearance

- **Eclipse**
- **Aqua Heartia**
- **Shadow Blade**

Choose one yourself, or use **AUTO** to switch with your skill: Aqua Heartia for Earth Drill and Stone Cannon, Shadow Blade for Atomic, and Eclipse for the others.

## Keep your builds safe

Destructive spells can change a large area. **Terrain destruction is enabled by default.**

Server owners can turn off permanent block damage for Earth Drill, Supernova, Stone Cannon, Atomic and Fuga. Earth Drill and Supernova also have separate choices for lifting blocks, so you can keep temporary block movement or use damage-only versions.

Damage, cooldowns and selected terrain ranges can be adjusted. Atomic's main blast and outer shockwaves have separate damage settings. Existing settings files automatically gain new options without replacing your choices.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Hold V** | Choose a skill while holding the wand |
| **Right-click** | Use the selected skill; hold to charge where required |
| **K** | Choose a wand appearance and adjust visual effects |
| **Shift while Spatial Path is active** | Use the temporary bridge |
| **Esc or F5 during Atomic or Fuga** | Skip its camera |
