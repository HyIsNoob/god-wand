# God Wand

[Wiki, controls and guides](https://god-wand-wiki.vercel.app/)

**One wand. Nine skills. Choose the spell, charge it, and decide when to release.**

Walk through mountains, pull enemies into a gravity orb, raise a drill from the ground, hold an Atomic blast until you are ready to let it go, draw a burning bow and fire Fuga's phoenix arrow, open a gate in the sky and rain light on an army, or crash a blue and a red orb together into Purple.

This mod contains intense flashing lights and visual effects that may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Player discretion is advised.

## Nine skills

### 1\. Spatial Path

Open a temporary path through solid terrain. The blocks return behind you. Use its shield against incoming projectiles, or form a temporary bridge across a gap.

### 2\. Singularity

Hold right-click to charge a gravity orb, then release to launch it. It pulls in nearby enemies and blocks before collapsing. Right-click again to trigger the collapse early. Displaced terrain returns afterward.

### 3\. Earth Drill

Gather nearby blocks into a spinning drill, then drive it into the ground. Server settings let you keep the block lift while turning off permanent terrain damage.

### 4\. Supernova

Hold right-click for seven seconds to unleash a black hole and a large explosion. Server owners can allow the block pull, the terrain destruction, both or neither.

### 5\. Stone Cannon

Charge a stone projectile and fire it through enemies. Block destruction on impact can be turned off separately.

### 6\. I am Atomic

Hold right-click as a fortress rises around you and energy gathers into your sword. **I AM. ATOMIC.** The screen goes dark and a heartbeat waits for you. **Let go to detonate:** a column of light sweeps the area, with shockwaves and purple wind, and leaves glowing marks on the ground. You can't move or be hurt while casting. Let go early to cancel.

### 7\. Fuga

Hold right-click to gather fire between your palms and draw a bow of flame. Aim, then release. The arrow becomes a phoenix; where it hits, a meteor falls and a pillar of fire erupts, setting enemies alight and slowing those caught underneath. Let go early to cancel.

### 8\. Silent Gate

Raise the wand: a magic circle covers the sky and a winged gate comes down through it. **Right-click again to give the command** (or wait): hundreds of circles rain light on every monster around you, and a golden beam finishes off the strongest. Sneak to stop it while the gate is still forming.

### 9\. Purple

Two orbs, one in each hand.

*   **Blue (hold left-click):** a gravity orb that follows your aim, pulling in and crushing enemies. Once it is ready, let go to throw it; it hangs where it stops.
*   **Red (hold right-click):** charge a red blast and release it to blow enemies away and tunnel through terrain.
*   **Fire a fully charged Red into a ready Blue:** the two merge into Purple, with its own cutscene, and a huge sphere erases everything in its path. It works across players: your Blue, a friend's Red.
*   **Fire any other Red into a Blue:** a smaller Purple explodes on the spot.
*   **Hold both buttons:** form a purple sphere in front of you, then let go to fire it straight ahead.

Blue and Red each have their own cooldown; Purple puts both on cooldown. More on the [wiki](https://god-wand-wiki.vercel.app/).

### Cutscenes

Atomic, Fuga, Silent Gate and Purple each have their own cutscene and soundtrack. Press Esc or F5 to skip the camera. Turn the cutscenes off in the settings menu (K), all at once or one by one.

## Pick your wand's appearance

*   **Eclipse**
*   **Aqua Heartia**
*   **Shadow Blade**
*   **Silent Witch Staff**

Choose one yourself, or use **AUTO** to switch with your skill: Aqua Heartia for Earth Drill and Stone Cannon, Shadow Blade for Atomic, Silent Witch Staff for Silent Gate, and Eclipse for the others.

## Keep your builds and pets safe

Destructive spells can change a large area. **Terrain destruction is enabled by default.**

*   **One switch turns off terrain damage for every skill.** Each skill also has its own switch, and Earth Drill and Supernova can keep lifting blocks without leaving permanent damage.
*   **Your pets are safe by default.** Spells don't hurt or push tamed animals, mobs with a name tag or mobs on a lead. Server owners can make spells hit everything, or monsters only.
*   **PvP can be turned off,** so spells never hurt other players.

Damage, cooldowns and terrain ranges can be adjusted for each skill. Existing settings files gain new options automatically and keep your choices.

## Controls

| Key                                                 | Action                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| <strong>Hold V</strong>                             | Choose a skill while holding the wand                 |
| <strong>Right-click</strong>                        | Use the selected skill; hold to charge where required |
| <strong>Left-click (Purple)</strong>                | Hold for Blue; hold with right-click for the Purple sphere |
| <strong>K</strong>                                  | Choose a wand appearance, cutscenes, effects and sound |
| <strong>Shift while Spatial Path is active</strong> | Use the temporary bridge                              |
| <strong>Right-click while Silent Gate waits</strong> | Command the gate to fire                             |
| <strong>Esc or F5 during a cutscene</strong>        | Skip its camera                                       |
